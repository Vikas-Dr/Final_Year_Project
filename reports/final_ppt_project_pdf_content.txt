Slide 1: AI-Powered Interview Evaluation & Resume Analysis System
- An intelligent recruiting system for resume screening and candidate evaluation.
- Helps recruiters reduce manual review work and improve decision-making.
- Combines resume analysis, identity verification, and interview assessment in one platform.

Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Manual review of resumes is slow, tiring, and inconsistent.
- Recruiters spend too much time comparing skills with job requirements.
- Fake candidates can sometimes enter interviews without proper verification.
- The project solves this using AI, automation, and secure identity checks.

Slide 3: Objectives
- Automatically analyze resumes and extract skills, education, and experience.
- Compare candidate profiles with job requirements using a match score.
- Verify identity using ID upload and selfie matching before the test begins.
- Generate structured interview reports for recruiters and evaluators.

Slide 4: Key Features
- Resume upload in PDF/DOCX/TXT format.
- Interview question generation based on job role.
- AI evaluation of technical and communication performance.
- Dashboard, candidate rankings, and downloadable reports.

Slide 5: Identity Verification
- Candidate uploads ID card and captures live face image.
- The system checks if both images match and validates identity.
- Access to the interview is only allowed after successful verification.
- This reduces cheating and fake participation.

Slide 6: Conclusion
- The project brings together resume analysis, hiring intelligence, and secure identity checks.
- It improves recruitment speed, fairness, and overall efficiency.
- The system is suitable for companies, colleges, and modern recruitment platforms.
- It is a strong final-year project with practical real-world value.
